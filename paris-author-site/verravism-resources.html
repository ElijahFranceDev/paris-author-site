For journalists, booksellers and educators

Short description: Verravism is a literary movement founded by P.M. France in 2026 that explores fractured certainty through storytelling. Its framework places character, text, and reader in deliberate tension, making shifts in belief consequential while preserving a comprehensible central narrative.

Founder credit: P.M. France, founding author of Verravism and author of Verravic Fiction and psychological thrillers.

Critical discussion and research

Verravism is a contemporary, founder-led framework. Its techniques overlap with established narratology, reader-response criticism, unreliable narration, and experimental literature. Comparative analysis, replication through original stories, and critical commentary are encouraged. Do not treat the movement’s self-description as proof of academic recognition.
