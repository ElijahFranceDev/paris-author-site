Smile For Me marks the next era of P.M. France: darker, more psychological, more unsettling, and connected to something much larger than a single book.

P.M. France is the current author identity behind a new creative direction built around psychological thrillers, dark fiction, unreliable truths, obsession, and stories that reward readers for paying attention.
