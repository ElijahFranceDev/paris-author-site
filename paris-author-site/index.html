Smile For Me

Psychological Thriller Dark Mystery Standalone Novel

She built a career finding the details everyone else missed. Then one investigation destroyed her credibility and sent her running from the life she knew. Years later, Simone Reed still cannot leave an unanswered question alone, especially when reality starts giving her reasons not to trust it.

Smile For Me marks the next era of P.M. France: darker, more psychological, more unsettling, and built to stay with the reader long after the final page.

P.M. France

P.M. France is the current author identity of the writer previously published as Paris M. France. The Quiet Days remains credited to Paris M. France, while new psychological thriller and horror fiction continues under P.M. France.

The work now centers on unreliable truths, obsession, memory, perception, and stories that reward readers for paying attention. The name changed because the creative direction changed. The author did not.
