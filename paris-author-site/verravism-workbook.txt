BETA READER QUESTIONNAIRE
1. What did you think was happening before the reveal?
2. Which evidence led you there?
3. What changed your understanding?
4. What felt unfair or accidentally confusing?
5. What action or relationship changed because of the discovery?
6. What did the ending resolve, and what stayed with you?

These are voluntary craft tools, not certification. Keep your own notes private unless you choose to share them.
