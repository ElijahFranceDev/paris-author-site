One Author. Two Publishing Names.

The name changed. The stories got darker.

P.M. France is a psychological thriller and psychological horror author whose fiction leans into memory, obsession, perception, identity, unreliable truths, and the uncomfortable space between what happened and what someone believes happened.

The earlier work The Quiet Days was published under the name Paris M. France. Beginning with Smile For Me, new fiction is published as P.M. France. Both names refer to the same author and the same developing catalog.

Previously published as Paris M. France

The Quiet Days remains credited to Paris M. France, while future psychological thriller and horror releases continue under P.M. France.

The work is built for readers who like stories that keep moving after the last page, where small details matter, certainty is dangerous, and answers tend to create better questions.
