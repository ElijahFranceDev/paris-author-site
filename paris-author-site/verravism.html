What is Verravism?

Verravism is a literary movement founded by P.M. France in 2026 that explores fractured certainty through storytelling. Verravic Fiction places a character's understanding, the information supplied by the narrative, and the reader's interpretation in deliberate tension. The change must have meaningful consequences, while the central human story remains traceable.

Verravism is not fiction that is hard to understand. It is fiction that makes understanding itself matter.

Who founded Verravism?

P.M. France, previously published as Paris M. France, is the founding author. The movement's project instructions are dated November 3, 2026. This public founding edition introduces the philosophy, its standards, and its invitation to writers. It is a founder-led movement, not a claim of established academic recognition.

The Three-Witness Principle

Each witness has different access to information: the character believes, the text presents or withholds evidence, and the reader draws an inference. Their meaningful difference is called the Witness Delta.

The character remembers. The text presents. The reader decides. None is guaranteed to be right.

The Five Movements of Certainty

Establish, Disturb, Fracture, Reconstruct, Remain. These are recurring narrative functions, not compulsory acts. Explore the Verravic Method and original examples.

Verravism across genres

Verravic Fiction can take the form of psychological thrillers, horror, romance, urban crime, family drama, fantasy, historical fiction, and more. These are complementary retail and literary categories. A connected fictional universe, unreliable narrator, supernatural element, hidden code, or twist ending is not required.

Explore P.M. France's fiction through The Quiet Days, Smile For Me, and the author catalog. The Calm Universe is a fictional continuity, distinct from Verravism.

Frequently asked questions

Is Verravism the same as psychological horror?

No. Psychological horror is one possible expression. Verravism describes a relationship between belief, evidence, interpretation, and consequence across genres.

Does a Verravic story have to confuse the reader?

No. Deliberate uncertainty should not obscure the basic causal and emotional story. The editorial law is: Fix the mistakes. Protect the discomfort.

Can other writers practice Verravism?

Yes. The framework is intended for other writers across publishers and settings, with room for criticism and independent interpretation.

Is every technique unique to Verravism?

No. Unreliable narration, experimental form, and related techniques predate Verravism. Its claim is an authored combination, terminology, ethical limits, and repeatable craft framework.
