# Frontline Forge Solutions Carrier Command Portal — Premium Service

## Client-facing position

The FFS Carrier Command Portal is a secure, carrier-specific operations and recordkeeping system provided to participating Frontline Forge Solutions clients. It gives carrier owners and authorized drivers organized access to load activity, documents, reports, invoices, fleet records, fuel and mileage information, support requests, and compliance reminders.

## Premium features

- Invite-only carrier-owner and driver accounts
- Carrier-specific data protection
- Active, in-transit, delivered, canceled, and not-taken load history
- Pickup, delivery, appointment, commodity, weight, equipment, driver, and truck details
- Rate confirmations, BOLs, PODs, receipts, agreements, W-9s, insurance, and factoring documents
- Driver BOL/POD uploads
- Trucks, drivers, CDL, medical card, registration, and insurance records
- Weekly, monthly, and custom PDF carrier reports
- Automated monthly and weekly report generation
- Dispatch invoice generation and fee breakdowns
- Fuel expense and mileage tracking
- Loaded-mile and deadhead visibility
- Document and compliance-expiration reminders
- Live portal updates
- Email-ready notifications when configured
- Carrier support-request tracking
- Timestamped activity history
- Premium FFS black-and-gold experience

## FFS sales language

“Your portal gives you one organized place to see the work FFS is supporting—loads, paperwork, reports, invoices, fleet records, fuel, mileage, and operational requests. It is designed to help you stay organized and maintain professional business records while FFS supports your dispatch and back-office workflow.”

## Positioning note

Frontline Forge Solutions provides carrier support, dispatch assistance, load communication, paperwork organization, reporting, and back-office support. The portal does not represent a guarantee of loads, rates, revenue, or business results, and FFS does not act as a freight broker through this service.
